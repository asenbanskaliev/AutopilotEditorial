# VS-144 Production Readiness Audit

## Release decision

BookStudio may be released only when an independent audit covers every required dimension and no unresolved high or critical finding remains. The machine-readable audit evidence is generated by `scripts/vs144_production_readiness_audit.py`.

## Security, privacy and secret handling

Credentials are ephemeral, masked in CI and excluded from checkpoints, logs, evidence and publication packages. Manuscripts and prompts are treated as confidential content. Operators must minimize personal data, control retention and confirm authorization before sending content to external model providers.

## Copyright and licensing

The software is distributed under the repository `LICENSE`. Users remain responsible for rights to prompts, manuscripts, quotations, illustrations, fonts, source material and generated publication assets. The workflow must not present generated content as proof of originality, ownership or freedom from third-party rights.

## Dependency and supply-chain risk

.NET versions are controlled through `global.json` and central package declarations. OpenCode is installed ephemerally at a pinned version in live workflows. Dependency updates require CI, security review and provenance checks.

## Accessibility and user experience

The no-command journey must expose status in plain language, preserve keyboard-operable controls, avoid color-only meaning and provide actionable recovery messages. Human approval, blocking and resume decisions remain available through the editorial control contract.

## Installation, observability and recovery

Linux and Windows CI validate build and installation paths. OpenTelemetry, structured progress events and normalized evidence provide operational visibility. SQLite checkpoints, idempotent artifact receipts and restart tests protect long-running editorial work against duplication and loss.

## KDP and release evidence

KDP readiness includes manuscript EPUB, print interior PDF, full-wrap cover PDF, metadata, publication checklist, layout checks, package manifest and SHA-256 verification. Passing these checks reduces upload errors but does not constitute approval by Amazon or legal advice.

## Residual risks accepted for this release

1. **Literal several-hour acceptance run:** VS-143 provides a strict gate requiring evidence for a run of at least two hours and 30,000 words, plus a live connected OpenCode and real-MCP sample. The pull-request CI sample is not itself the literal multi-hour production run. Before a major public release, operations must supply and archive a full-scale evidence record that passes the VS-143 gate.
2. **Model and provider variability:** Free model availability, latency and output quality may change. Failover and quality gates reduce impact but cannot eliminate provider drift.
3. **KDP policy changes:** Amazon KDP requirements can change after a release. Operators must confirm current KDP requirements before publication.
4. **Human rights review:** Automated originality, factual and copyright checks cannot replace qualified human or legal review for high-risk publications.

These are medium or lower operational risks. They are accepted only with the controls above and must be reconsidered for each production release.
