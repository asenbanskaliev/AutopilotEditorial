# VS-110 — Manuscript assembly

## Intent

Create one durable canonical manuscript source from the exact approved editorial, research, rights, visual and accessibility authorities required for downstream rendering.

## Behaviors

1. An assembly identifies workspace, project, locale, target channels, actor, deterministic request fingerprint and exact upstream revisions and digests.
2. Front matter, body and back matter are represented as explicitly typed ordered sections with stable identifiers.
3. Chapters, scenes, paragraphs, citations, footnotes, endnotes, tables, figures, captions and accessibility alternatives are bound deterministically to their approved authorities.
4. Visual assets must reference current VS-101 registry authority, PASS VS-103 audit evidence and approved VS-105 accessibility evidence; cover authority is recorded but excluded from interior ordering unless explicitly permitted.
5. Claims, citations, rights and AI-provenance disclosures must reference current approved VS-091 through VS-095 evidence where applicable.
6. Assembly validation rejects missing, duplicate, stale, cross-workspace, digest-mismatched, unordered or unapproved source material.
7. Canonical content and manifest digests are deterministic for the same authoritative inputs and ordering.
8. Exact replay is idempotent; conflicting reuse and stale revisions fail without duplicate assembly revisions or events.
9. Assembly state, source bindings, sections, validation findings, decisions, receipts, append-only history and Outbox messages are committed atomically and reconstructed from SQLite after restart.
10. Approval freezes an immutable canonical manuscript revision for VS-111 and later renderers; changes require a superseding assembly revision.

## Invariants

- No approved canonical manuscript references stale, missing, cross-boundary or digest-mismatched authority.
- Structural ordering is explicit, total and deterministic.
- The canonical manifest identifies every included source and every excluded optional source.
- Renderers cannot mutate the approved canonical manuscript.
- Exact replay cannot duplicate durable work or Outbox effects.
- A failed transaction cannot expose partial manuscript state.

## Gates

- Typed assembly, authority, section, content-node, source-binding, finding, decision and state contracts.
- Exact authority and lineage enforcement across authoring, editorial, research, rights, visual and accessibility slices.
- Deterministic structure, ordering, manifest and digest generation.
- Approval, repair, rejection and supersession governance.
- Replay, optimistic concurrency, rollback, restart recovery and workspace isolation.
- Append-only history and deterministic exactly-once Outbox.
- Dual TDD GREEN, Auditoría M, Meta-Audit, RetroSpec and complete same-head CI.
