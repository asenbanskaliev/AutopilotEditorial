# VS-102 — Image adapters

## Intent

Provide a durable, provider-neutral image-adapter orchestration boundary that can generate or ingest visual assets and register every accepted result through the authoritative VS-101 asset registry.

## Behaviors

1. A request declares workspace, project, exact approved/current VS-100 visual-brief authority, requested asset type, provider adapter, operation mode, capability requirements, prompt or manual-source identity, generation parameters, output policy, actor, and deterministic request fingerprint.
2. Supported adapter classes include ComfyUI, local engines, remote providers, and manual ingestion.
3. Adapter capabilities are explicit and versioned, including generation, variation, inpainting, image-to-image, upscaling, deterministic seed support, cancellation, and manual import.
4. Provider-native responses are normalized into attempts, outputs, warnings, errors, usage, provenance, rights hints, technical metadata, and immutable content digests.
5. Every accepted output is registered through VS-101 with the exact workspace, project, visual-brief authority, provenance, rights, accessibility, storage, digest, and relationship context.
6. Unsupported capability, provider mismatch, unsafe path, invalid media, stale authority, cross-boundary access, digest conflict, conflicting replay, cancellation race, and partial provider failure fail closed.
7. Request, attempts, receipts, outputs, provider evidence, append-only history, asset-registration linkage, and deterministic Outbox messages are atomic and restart-safe.
8. Exact replay is idempotent; conflicting request reuse and optimistic-concurrency violations produce no duplicate provider or registry side effects.
9. Retry policy is bounded and distinguishes transient provider failure from permanent validation or governance failure.
10. Authoritative reads reconstruct request and attempt state from persistence after restart.

## Invariants

- No completed adapter request exists without exact current visual-brief authority and authoritative VS-101 registration for every accepted output.
- Provider-specific payloads never become the domain contract or bypass normalized validation.
- Workspace, project, brief, storage root, provider identity, capability set, provenance chain, and rights context cannot be mixed.
- Failed or cancelled attempts cannot create partially registered assets, receipts, history, or events.
- Exact replay cannot duplicate provider invocation intent or durable asset registration.
- Manual ingestion is governed by the same digest, path, provenance, rights, accessibility, and technical-validation rules as generated output.

## Gates

- Typed provider-neutral request, capability, attempt, result, failure, usage, and provenance contracts.
- Explicit ComfyUI, local, remote, and manual adapter boundaries.
- Exact VS-100 authority and VS-101 registry integration.
- Safe path, immutable digest, media metadata, provider evidence, rights, accessibility, and technical validation.
- Bounded retry, cancellation, replay, concurrency, rollback, restart, and workspace isolation.
- Append-only history and exactly-once Outbox.
- DUAL_GREEN, Auditoría M, Meta-Audit, RetroSpec, and complete same-head CI.
