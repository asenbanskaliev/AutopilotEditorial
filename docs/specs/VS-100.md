# VS-100 — Visual briefs

## Intent

Create durable, versioned visual briefs and continuity authority for every visual asset, authorized by the exact approved and current VS-095 legal-risk case.

## Behaviors

1. A brief declares workspace, project, brief type, subject/content version, exact VS-095 authority, actor, target channel, dimensions, crop and safe-zone constraints, and causal snapshot.
2. Supported brief types include cover, chapter, scene, character, location, object, diagram, and marketing asset.
3. Briefs preserve art direction, composition, subject identity, continuity constraints, style, palette, typography intent, accessibility intent, prohibited elements, and evidence.
4. Reusable continuity references preserve authoritative character, location, object, motif, and series identity across briefs.
5. Only approved, current, non-stale VS-095 authority may authorize creation or approval.
6. Approval is blocked when required fields, continuity references, accessibility intent, legal constraints, or review evidence are incomplete.
7. Content, legal authority, continuity reference, dimensions, channel, or policy drift marks the brief stale.
8. History is append-only and reconstructs create, revise, review, approve, return-to-repair, revoke, reopen, and stale transitions.
9. Exact request replay is idempotent; conflicting reuse, optimistic concurrency violations, cross-workspace access, or authority mismatch fail without partial writes.
10. Brief, continuity references, receipts, history, and deterministic Outbox messages are atomic and restart-safe.

## Invariants

- No visual brief exists without exact approved and current VS-095 authority.
- A brief cannot mix workspaces, projects, subjects, content versions, legal authorities, channels, or continuity contexts.
- APPROVED cannot exist with missing mandatory information, unresolved blocking findings, stale authority, or incomplete review evidence.
- Failed transitions leave no partial brief, continuity reference, receipt, history, or event.
- Exact replay does not duplicate durable side effects.

## Gates

- Exact VS-095 authority and drift detection.
- Complete typed brief and evidence-bearing continuity model.
- Dimensions, crop, safe-zone, typography, accessibility, and prohibited-element validation.
- Fail-closed review and approval rules.
- Replay, concurrency, rollback, restart, and workspace isolation.
- Exactly-once Outbox.
- DUAL_GREEN, Auditoría M, Meta-Audit, RetroSpec, and complete same-head CI.
