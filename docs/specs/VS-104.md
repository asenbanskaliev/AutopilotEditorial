# VS-104 — Cover workflow

## Intent

Provide a durable, authority-bound workflow for composing, reviewing and approving complete book-cover variants across print, ebook, thumbnail and channel-specific outputs.

## Behaviors

1. A cover project identifies workspace, project, exact VS-100 visual-brief authority, exact VS-101 asset revisions and digests, VS-102 adapter provenance, VS-103 visual-audit outcomes, target channel, trim geometry, typography content, actor and deterministic request fingerprint.
2. Cover variants model front cover, spine, back cover, ebook crop, thumbnail and channel/genre derivatives with explicit lineage.
3. Composition plans govern title, subtitle, author, series, imprint, blurb, barcode/ISBN zones, image placements, typography hierarchy, safe zones, bleed, spine width and export geometry.
4. Candidate assets must be authoritative, approved and backed by passing or explicitly governed VS-103 audit evidence before selection.
5. Thumbnail legibility, genre/channel fitness, typography hierarchy, contrast, crop and geometry checks are deterministic and evidence-bearing.
6. Decisions support select, approve, return-to-repair, reject and supersede with scoped authority, rationale and immutable evidence.
7. Exact replay is idempotent; conflicting reuse, stale authority, stale revision and cross-boundary access fail without duplicate variants, decisions or events.
8. Cover projects, variants, placements, evidence, decisions, receipts, append-only history and deterministic Outbox messages are atomic and restart-safe.
9. Authoritative reads reconstruct the current cover state from SQLite after restart and remain workspace/project isolated.
10. Completion requires exact current upstream authority, complete geometry and typography evidence, and an approved selected variant for every required channel.

## Invariants

- No approved cover references stale, missing, cross-workspace or digest-mismatched upstream authority.
- Unapproved assets or assets lacking acceptable VS-103 audit evidence cannot be selected.
- Spine, bleed, barcode/ISBN and safe-zone geometry cannot be omitted or bypassed for print variants.
- Thumbnail and channel variants retain explicit lineage to their approved source composition.
- Failed or rejected candidates cannot create an accepted cover decision or duplicate Outbox effect.
- Exact replay cannot duplicate durable cover work.

## Gates

- Typed cover-project, variant, placement, geometry, typography, evidence, decision and state contracts.
- Exact VS-100 through VS-103 authority and lineage enforcement.
- Deterministic geometry, safe-zone, thumbnail, genre/channel and typography validation.
- Selection, approval, repair, rejection and supersession governance.
- Replay, concurrency, rollback, restart and workspace isolation.
- Append-only history and exactly-once Outbox.
- Dual TDD GREEN, Auditoría M, Meta-Audit, RetroSpec and complete same-head CI.
