# VS-119 — Language governance and locale enforcement

## Intent

Guarantee that every AI-generated, editorial and production-facing text uses the book language and regional locale selected by the project, independently from the UI language or the language used internally by prompts.

## Behaviors

1. A project owns one immutable `BookLanguageTag` expressed as a canonical BCP-47 tag; UI language remains a separate preference and cannot alter book content language.
2. The active language authority identifies workspace, project, policy revision, canonical language tag, regional variant, allowed secondary-language scopes, actor and deterministic policy fingerprint.
3. Planning, outline, scene generation, rewriting, copyediting, metadata and production requests must bind the exact current language authority and its digest.
4. Every provider-neutral AI invocation compiles an explicit language contract containing required output language, locale conventions, forbidden unintended switching and governed exceptions.
5. Language-policy identity, version, compiled instruction digest, input digest and output digest are explicit and reproducible.
6. Generated text is evaluated by deterministic language and locale checks. Findings identify stable rule, severity, span/location, detected language, confidence, expected language and evidence digest.
7. Material drift from the required language, incompatible regional spelling or unauthorized mixed-language passages fails closed and prevents acceptance.
8. Quotations, proper nouns, citations and intentionally multilingual passages require explicit bounded exceptions with scope, reason, evidence, expiry or completion boundary and approval.
9. Failed language validation may trigger a bounded retry using the same immutable policy; conflicting replay, stale policy, cross-workspace authority or changed prompt policy must fail without duplicate effects.
10. Policies, compiled contracts, validations, findings, exceptions, decisions, replay receipts, append-only history and Outbox messages are committed atomically and reconstructed from SQLite after restart.
11. Approval freezes one immutable language-conformance evidence digest for downstream use; changes require a superseding policy or validation revision.

## Invariants

- UI language and book language are distinct authorities.
- No accepted generated content can reference a stale, missing, cross-workspace or digest-mismatched language policy.
- Identical governed inputs, policy version and detector versions produce deterministic evidence.
- Provider choice cannot weaken the language contract.
- Exact replay cannot duplicate validations, retries or Outbox effects.
- Transaction failure cannot expose partially accepted language state.

## Initial locale profiles

- `es-ES`: Spanish, Spain conventions.
- `es-MX`: Spanish, Mexico conventions.
- `en-US`: English, United States conventions.
- `en-GB`: English, United Kingdom conventions.

The design remains extensible to other valid BCP-47 tags without provider-specific branching.

## Gates

- Typed language authority, compiled contract, validation, finding, exception, retry, decision and state contracts.
- Canonical BCP-47 validation and locale-profile resolution.
- Exact language-authority propagation to every AI and editorial generation boundary.
- Deterministic provider-neutral prompt contract and evidence digests.
- Fail-closed post-generation language and locale validation.
- Governed mixed-language exceptions.
- Replay, optimistic concurrency, rollback, restart recovery and workspace isolation.
- Append-only history and deterministic exactly-once Outbox.
- Dual TDD GREEN, Auditoría M, Meta-Audit, RetroSpec and complete same-head CI.
