# VS-112 — Print PDF renderer

## Intent

Render a deterministic, print-ready PDF artifact from one exact approved upstream production authority while preserving canonical manuscript lineage and preventing geometry, typography, image or accessibility defects from reaching approval.

## Behaviors

1. A print render request identifies workspace, project, exact upstream artifact/revision/digests, trim size, bleed, margins, binding direction, paper profile, locale, actor and deterministic request fingerprint.
2. The renderer requires current approved VS-111 authority and rejects stale, missing, cross-workspace, digest-mismatched or non-approved input.
3. Page boxes, pagination, folios, front/body/back matter transitions, recto/verso rules and blank pages are explicit and deterministic.
4. Fonts must be embedded or governed by an approved substitution policy; missing glyphs, prohibited embedding or nondeterministic fallback fail closed.
5. Images retain exact source lineage, approved rights, color profile, effective DPI, dimensions, captions and accessibility alternatives where applicable.
6. PDF metadata, output intent, document identifiers, timestamps and producer data are generated deterministically from governed inputs.
7. Validation records geometry, typography, image, color, link, metadata, accessibility and PDF preflight-compatible findings; blocking findings prevent approval.
8. Exact replay is idempotent; conflicting reuse and stale revisions fail without duplicate render revisions or events.
9. Request, page manifest, resource bindings, findings, decisions, receipts, append-only history and Outbox messages are committed atomically and reconstructed from SQLite after restart.
10. Approval freezes an immutable print artifact digest for VS-113 and later production slices; changes require a superseding render revision.

## Invariants

- No approved print PDF references stale or mismatched upstream authority.
- Page geometry and resource placement are deterministic for identical governed inputs.
- Upstream authority is read-only.
- Every embedded font and image has exact identity, rights and integrity evidence.
- Exact replay cannot duplicate durable work or Outbox effects.
- Failed validation or transaction cannot expose an approved partial PDF.

## Gates

- Typed print request, authority, geometry, page, resource, finding, decision and state contracts.
- Exact VS-111 authority enforcement.
- Deterministic pagination, page boxes, metadata and artifact digest.
- Font embedding, image DPI/color and PDF preflight-compatible validation evidence.
- Approval, repair, rejection and supersession governance.
- Replay, optimistic concurrency, rollback, restart recovery and workspace isolation.
- Append-only history and deterministic exactly-once Outbox.
- Dual TDD GREEN, Auditoría M, Meta-Audit, RetroSpec and complete same-head CI.
