# VS-105 — Visual accessibility

## Intent

Provide durable, authority-bound accessibility assessment and approval for visual assets, approved covers and downstream rendered image usage.

## Behaviors

1. An accessibility case identifies workspace, project, exact VS-101 asset authority, VS-103 audit evidence, VS-104 approved cover authority where applicable, intended channel, locale, actor and deterministic request fingerprint.
2. Every non-decorative visual receives concise alt text; complex visuals may require a long description; decorative visuals require an explicit governed classification.
3. Text embedded in imagery requires an equivalent textual alternative and evidence that essential meaning is not available only as pixels.
4. Contrast assessment records foreground, background, measured ratio, applicable threshold, large-text status and evidence digest.
5. Reading-order and association intent are explicit for visual sequences, captions and nearby prose.
6. Automated findings and human review are normalized into deterministic decisions supporting approve, repair, reject and supersede.
7. Exact replay is idempotent; conflicting reuse, stale authority, stale revision and cross-boundary access fail without duplicate assessments, decisions or events.
8. Cases, findings, descriptions, contrast evidence, decisions, receipts, append-only history and deterministic Outbox messages are atomic and restart-safe.
9. Authoritative reads reconstruct current accessibility state from SQLite after restart and remain workspace/project isolated.
10. Completion requires current upstream authority, complete required alternatives, passing contrast or an explicit governed exception, and final approval.

## Invariants

- No approved accessibility result references stale, missing, cross-workspace or digest-mismatched upstream authority.
- Non-decorative visuals cannot be approved without required textual alternatives.
- Decorative classification cannot be inferred silently or used to bypass meaningful-image requirements.
- Essential text or meaning cannot remain available only inside an image.
- Contrast failures cannot be converted to PASS without governed evidence and permitted authority.
- Exact replay cannot duplicate durable accessibility work.

## Gates

- Typed case, visual use, description, contrast, finding, decision and state contracts.
- Exact VS-101, VS-103 and VS-104 authority and lineage enforcement.
- Deterministic alt-text, decorative, long-description, text-in-image, contrast and reading-order validation.
- Approval, repair, rejection and supersession governance.
- Replay, concurrency, rollback, restart and workspace isolation.
- Append-only history and exactly-once Outbox.
- Dual TDD GREEN, Auditoría M, Meta-Audit, RetroSpec and complete same-head CI.
