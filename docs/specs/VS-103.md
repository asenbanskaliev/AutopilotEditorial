# VS-103 — Visual audits

## Intent

Provide a durable technical and semantic audit workflow for visual assets governed by the exact current VS-100 visual brief, authoritative VS-101 asset registry state, and VS-102 adapter provenance.

## Behaviors

1. An audit request identifies workspace, project, asset, exact asset revision/digest, exact visual-brief revision/digest, audit policy/version, requested technical and semantic checks, actor and deterministic request fingerprint.
2. Technical checks cover media integrity, dimensions, resolution/DPI, color profile, transparency, file size, corruption, crop and safe-zone constraints.
3. Semantic checks cover visual-brief conformance, subject identity, continuity references, prohibited elements, genre/channel fitness, provenance/rights completeness and accessibility prerequisites.
4. Every check produces a deterministic result with policy identity, outcome, severity, confidence, evidence, evidence digest and optional repair recommendation.
5. Findings aggregate into pass, repair-required, blocked or human-review-required outcomes without allowing partial or unknown results to pass.
6. Human decisions, waivers and overrides require scoped authority, rationale, evidence, expiry and immutable audit linkage; they cannot suppress legal, rights, provenance, stale-authority or cross-boundary failures.
7. Exact replay is idempotent; conflicting request reuse and stale revision fail without duplicate checks, decisions or events.
8. Audit runs, checks, findings, evidence, decisions, receipts, append-only history and deterministic Outbox messages are atomic and restart-safe.
9. Authoritative reads reconstruct audit state from SQLite after restart and remain workspace/project isolated.
10. Completion requires exact current VS-100 and VS-101 authority and complete check coverage for the selected policy.

## Invariants

- No passing audit exists for a stale, missing, cross-workspace or digest-mismatched visual brief or asset.
- Unknown, skipped, partial or unevidenced required checks never count as PASS.
- Semantic provider payloads never become the domain contract or bypass normalized evidence.
- Waivers are explicit, scoped, expiring and cannot override non-waivable governance failures.
- Failed or cancelled audit attempts cannot create an accepted decision or duplicate Outbox effect.
- Exact replay cannot duplicate durable audit work.

## Gates

- Typed audit request, policy, check, finding, evidence, decision, waiver and state contracts.
- Exact VS-100 brief and VS-101 asset authority; VS-102 provenance linkage.
- Technical and semantic check normalization with deterministic evidence digests.
- Fail-closed aggregation, human escalation and bounded waiver governance.
- Replay, concurrency, rollback, restart and workspace isolation.
- Append-only history and exactly-once Outbox.
- Dual TDD GREEN, Auditoría M, Meta-Audit, RetroSpec and complete same-head CI.
