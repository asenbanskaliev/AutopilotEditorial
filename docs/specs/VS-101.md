# VS-101 — Asset registry

## Intent

Create a durable, versioned visual-asset registry authorized by the exact approved and current VS-100 visual brief.

## Behaviors

1. An asset declares workspace, project, asset type, exact VS-100 visual-brief authority, actor, source adapter, storage location, media format, dimensions, color profile, cryptographic digest, causal snapshot, and generation parameters.
2. Supported asset types include cover, chapter illustration, scene image, character reference, location reference, object reference, diagram, marketing asset, and derived rendition.
3. Assets preserve provenance, rights and license evidence, source model or provider, prompt/input lineage, accessibility metadata, technical validation evidence, and parent/superseded relationships.
4. Only approved, current, non-stale VS-100 authority may authorize registration or approval.
5. Registration validates safe storage paths, immutable content digest, workspace/project/brief boundaries, supported media metadata, and artifact-store integrity.
6. Approval is blocked when provenance, rights/license evidence, accessibility metadata, technical validation, digest integrity, or brief authority is incomplete or stale.
7. Brief, content, rights, storage, digest, technical policy, or provenance drift marks the asset stale or quarantined as appropriate.
8. History is append-only and reconstructs ingest, validate, approve, supersede, quarantine, repair, revoke, and stale transitions.
9. Exact request replay is idempotent; conflicting reuse, digest collision, optimistic concurrency violations, unsafe paths, cross-workspace access, or authority mismatch fail without partial writes.
10. Asset state, receipts, history, provenance, rights evidence, technical validations, relationships, and deterministic Outbox messages are atomic and restart-safe.

## Invariants

- No asset exists without exact approved and current VS-100 visual-brief authority.
- An asset cannot mix workspaces, projects, briefs, subjects, storage roots, provenance chains, or rights contexts.
- APPROVED cannot exist with missing provenance, rights/license evidence, accessibility metadata, technical validation, digest integrity, or current brief authority.
- Content digest and canonical storage identity are immutable for an asset version.
- Failed transitions leave no partial asset, evidence, relationship, receipt, history, or event.
- Exact replay does not duplicate durable side effects.

## Gates

- Exact VS-100 authority and drift detection.
- Complete typed asset, provenance, rights, accessibility, and technical-validation model.
- Safe path, storage-root, digest, format, dimensions, and color-profile validation.
- Fail-closed approval, quarantine, repair, supersede, revoke, and stale rules.
- Replay, concurrency, rollback, restart, artifact integrity, and workspace isolation.
- Exactly-once Outbox.
- DUAL_GREEN, Auditoría M, Meta-Audit, RetroSpec, and complete same-head CI.
