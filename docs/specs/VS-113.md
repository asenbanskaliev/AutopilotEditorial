# VS-113 — DOCX renderer

## Intent

Render one deterministic, editable DOCX package from the exact approved VS-112 production authority while preserving canonical structure, lineage, accessibility and reviewability.

## Behaviors

1. A DOCX render request identifies workspace, project, exact upstream render/revision/digests, locale, template profile, compatibility target, actor and deterministic request fingerprint.
2. The renderer requires current approved VS-112 authority and rejects stale, missing, cross-workspace, digest-mismatched or non-approved input.
3. Sections, headings, paragraphs, lists, tables, figures, captions, notes, page breaks, headers, footers and styles are mapped explicitly and deterministically.
4. Package parts, relationships, content types, numbering, styles and document properties use stable identities and ordering.
5. Fonts, images and embedded resources retain exact identity, rights, integrity and accessibility evidence; unsafe external relationships fail closed.
6. Metadata, timestamps, revision identifiers and producer data are derived deterministically from governed inputs.
7. Validation records package, structure, style, relationship, compatibility, accessibility and editability findings; blocking findings prevent approval.
8. Exact replay is idempotent; conflicting reuse and stale revisions fail without duplicate render revisions or events.
9. Request, package manifest, part bindings, findings, decisions, receipts, append-only history and Outbox messages are committed atomically and reconstructed from SQLite after restart.
10. Approval freezes an immutable DOCX artifact digest for VS-114 and later production slices; changes require a superseding render revision.

## Invariants

- No approved DOCX references stale or mismatched upstream authority.
- Package structure and part ordering are deterministic for identical governed inputs.
- Upstream authority is read-only.
- Every relationship and embedded resource is bounded, safe and attributable.
- Exact replay cannot duplicate durable work or Outbox effects.
- Failed validation or transaction cannot expose an approved partial DOCX.

## Gates

- Typed request, authority, section, part, relationship, resource, finding, decision and state contracts.
- Exact VS-112 authority enforcement.
- Deterministic Open Packaging Convention manifest, styles, numbering and metadata.
- Relationship safety, resource rights/integrity and accessibility validation evidence.
- Approval, repair, rejection and supersession governance.
- Replay, optimistic concurrency, rollback, restart recovery and workspace isolation.
- Append-only history and deterministic exactly-once Outbox.
- Dual TDD GREEN, Auditoría M, Meta-Audit, RetroSpec and complete same-head CI.
