# VS-117 — Proof workflow

## Intent

Validate the exact approved VS-116 KDP package through governed digital-galley and physical-proof review before professional release.

## Behaviors

1. A proof request identifies workspace, project, exact VS-116 package/revision/evidence digest, package digest, proof type, locale, reviewer, actor and deterministic request fingerprint.
2. The workflow requires the current approved VS-116 authority and rejects stale, missing, cross-workspace, digest-mismatched or non-approved input.
3. Digital galley and physical proof reviews use versioned checklists covering trim, margins, bleed, pagination, typography, images, color, links, metadata, cover alignment and readability.
4. Findings use stable identities, severity, location, rule, annotation/evidence digest, remediation status and reviewer disposition.
5. Blocking findings prevent approval; corrections create a new governed cycle bound to a superseding package revision rather than mutating frozen evidence.
6. Physical-proof receipt records provider/order reference, received date, inspected artifact identity and reviewer attestation without inventing external acceptance.
7. Exact replay is idempotent; conflicting reuse and stale revisions fail without duplicate proof cycles or Outbox effects.
8. Requests, checklist executions, findings, annotations, receipts, decisions, append-only history and Outbox messages are committed atomically and reconstructed from SQLite after restart.
9. Approval freezes one immutable proof evidence digest for VS-118; changes require a superseding proof cycle.

## Invariants

- No approved proof references stale or mismatched VS-116 authority.
- Approved proof contains no unresolved blocking finding.
- Upstream package artifacts are read-only and verified by digest.
- Exact replay cannot duplicate durable state or Outbox effects.
- Failed review, decision or transaction cannot expose partially approved proof state.

## Gates

- Typed request, authority, checklist, finding, receipt, decision and state contracts.
- Exact VS-116 authority enforcement.
- Deterministic checklist execution and evidence digests.
- Fail-closed approval and governed correction-cycle semantics.
- Replay, optimistic concurrency, rollback, restart recovery and workspace isolation.
- Append-only history and deterministic exactly-once Outbox.
- Dual TDD GREEN, Auditoría M, Meta-Audit, RetroSpec and complete same-head CI.
