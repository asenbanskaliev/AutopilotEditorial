# VS-138 — Real Commercial Book Creation

## Objective

Replace deterministic acceptance prose with production OpenCode adapters that create an original chapter plan and unique long-form chapters from one natural-language idea.

## Acceptance

- The planner returns a contiguous exact chapter plan with premise, ending promise, goals and continuity anchors.
- Every chapter is generated by an approved model and preserves provider/model/prompt evidence.
- Chapters reject placeholders, model wrappers, insufficient length, excessive sentence repetition and duplicate chapter hashes.
- The book-level policy rejects fewer than eight chapters, insufficient total words and duplicate chapters.
- Durable full-book orchestration, checkpoints, literary gates and KDP packaging remain reusable from VS-132 to VS-137.
- A later long-running live proof in VS-143 must create tens of thousands of real model-generated words; VS-138 establishes and tests the production adapters and commercial-content invariants.

## Security

Prompts never persist credentials. Model execution metadata stores hashes and model identifiers only. Failure messages do not include prompt bodies or secrets.
