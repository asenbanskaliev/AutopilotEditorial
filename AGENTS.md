# BookStudio Development Contract

## Final objective

Complete the entire program. Do not stop at the MVP.

## Method

- SDD.
- Dual TDD.
- M-Audit.
- Vertical slices.
- Product Flow Gate.

## Rules

1. Read `docs/execution/EXECUTION_STATUS.md`.
2. Work on one READY slice.
3. Define contracts before production code.
4. Preserve RED-I and RED-E evidence.
5. Do not weaken confirmed tests without TestChangeRequest.
6. Do not create orphan tools, endpoints, events, screens or jobs.
7. Do not require hidden commands in product journeys.
8. Do not self-approve.
9. Generate evidence and RetroSpec.
10. Update status and traceability.
11. Open a draft PR.
12. After merge, select the next READY slice.
13. Completion requires `FULL_PROGRAM_COMPLETE`.
